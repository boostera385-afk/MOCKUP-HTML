How it works

01 · Upload one photo

Choose a clear photo showing the complete roofline. Avoid panoramas, heavy obstructions, screenshots and very dark photos. Daytime photos are welcome.

02 · Generate the night view

Click Generate to create one nighttime image with multicolor scalloped lighting. It usually takes under a minute, but can take longer when the service is busy.

03 · Compare and download

Switch between the original and the night view, use the comparison slider, and download your preview.

About accuracy

The AI is instructed to keep your home's geometry, but exact preservation is not guaranteed. Inspect the result; another attempt can give a different result.

Photo privacy

Your photo

Your browser re-encodes the photo to remove embedded metadata such as GPS location. When you click Generate, the photo is sent to Vercel AI Gateway and its image model provider. Visible details such as house numbers remain in the image; only upload photos you are allowed to use.

Storage

Photos, previews and the allowance are kept in the server's memory only. Idle sessions are removed after two hours, and everything is removed when the server restarts. A browser cookie identifies your session.

Limits

Each browser gets a limited number of previews per day, plus a cap on failed or stopped attempts. Downloads don't use your allowance.
